Open Glass is my open-source take on a company-wide AI workspace, where every employee works with agents that share what the company knows.

**What I'm designing**
- **Layered memory** - personal, project, team and company - so each agent sees the right context.
- **Provenance:** every fact keeps its source, and revoking a source removes what was learned from it.
- **Review states**, so people can correct what agents believe instead of fighting it.
- **Periodic synthesis** that keeps shared knowledge current without anyone curating it.

It's in the design phase - architecture and memory-design docs, no app yet.
