Pal is a personal agent that learns how you work. It researches the web, keeps your notes, bookmarks, people and meetings in tables it creates as needed, and gets better every run through a learning machine - with a strict line between your data and what it learns about doing the job.

**What I built**
- The design and the learning-machine primitives: learning stores, team support, and guards that stop agents looping when they update memory.
- Deployment on Docker, AWS (with EFS so its database survives deploys) and Railway, with authenticated Exa MCP for research.
