Dash answers business questions in plain English with grounded SQL. Every answer is grounded in layered context - table schemas, validated queries, business rules and what it has learned - and it runs as an analyst/engineer pair with read-only and read-write access.

**Built to run in your cloud.** Any model, Postgres for data and learnings, JWT auth, Slack and an eval suite for accuracy, routing and safety.

**What I built**
- The architecture: layered context, the analyst/engineer split and the learning loop.
- The production setup for running Dash in a team's Slack workspace.
