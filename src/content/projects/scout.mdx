Scout is a company-intelligence agent. Instead of chunking and embedding everything up front, it **navigates** a company's sources - Slack, Google Drive, a wiki, a CRM and MCP servers - and keeps its own wiki and CRM up to date as it learns.

**Built to run in your cloud.** Any model, Postgres for its wiki and CRM, JWT auth in production, and Slack as its home - each thread is a session. CRM writes are locked to Scout's own schema by the database, not by a prompt.

**What I built**
- The architecture: one agent, one `query_<source>` tool per source, each backed by its own sub-agent.
- Context providers in Agno - including Slack, Gmail and Calendar - with live streaming of sub-agent work.
- Scout's first evaluation suite, and the migration onto Agno's shared context-provider library - which also fixed a user-ID leak into CRM rows.
