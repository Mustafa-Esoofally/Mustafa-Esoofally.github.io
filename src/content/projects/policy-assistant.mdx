**The problem:** every large firm has the answers in its handbooks - leave, travel, expenses, tax, HR rules - but nobody can find them, so the same questions land in HR's inbox again and again.

**What it does**
- Employees ask in plain English: *"How many leave days do I have?"*, *"I'm flying from New York to Chicago next week - can I book this flight, and which hotels can I use?"*
- A multi-agent system: a coordinator routes each question to specialized agents, each running on its own slice of company knowledge - handbooks, HR guidelines, travel and expense policy, tax documents, Notion pages and PDFs.
- Each specialist retrieves with Agno's knowledge features - agentic search and full RAG over the document embeddings - and a reasoning SQL agent handles structured data like leave balances.
- Answers stay grounded in the firm's own policy and point back to the source, instead of guessing.

**Where it ran:** a global asset manager, a top-10 accounting firm and an AmLaw 50 law firm.
