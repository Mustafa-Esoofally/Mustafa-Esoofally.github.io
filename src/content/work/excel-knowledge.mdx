- Split Excel out of the CSV reader into its own reader for `.xlsx` and `.xls`, with automatic routing by file type.
- Choose sheets by name or index, skip hidden sheets, and ingest row-by-row or sheet-by-sheet.
- Every chunk keeps its workbook, sheet and row metadata, so answers can point back to where they came from.
- Part of a wider knowledge-hardening pass: deterministic chunk IDs, crawl de-duplication and an embedder memory fix.
